02Study 02  ·  BMW Z4 (E89)

The V8 Coupé

Proposed · S65 V8 · Manual

Concept render Nothing shown here has been built yet.

Nicky Catsburg, who won Spa in one, still calls the Z4 GT3 his favourite racing car. We wanted to see it as a road car.

Summary

The short version

The racer
The Z4 GT3, which won the Dubai 24 Hours outright in 2011 and the Spa 24 Hours in 2015.
What we change
A vented bonnet, wider arches, a deeper front bumper, a small ducktail and a rear diffuser.
What it is for
A small two-seater with a V8 and three pedals.
Settled
Nothing yet. The V8 and the manual gearbox are what we would like to fit.
What exists
Renders, design drawings, and cabin and colour studies. No blueprint or engineering yet.

Background

A customer racer with a famous voice

These photographs show the original cars and were taken by other people. Each one is credited underneath.

BMW launched the Z4 GT3 in March 2010 for privateer teams, and it won in its first season in the FIA GT3 European Championship. In January 2011, Team Schubert took it to outright victory at the Dubai 24 Hours.

In 2015, on its last major appearance before the M6 GT3 replaced it, the number 46 Marc VDS car won the 24 Hours of Spa. Nicky Catsburg, one of its drivers, still calls it his favourite racing car, small and nimble, and loud and beautiful.

BMW never sold a Z4 with a V8. In this study it gets one.

The car

A V8 in a small coupé

The GT3 car ran a racing V8 developed from the M3's S65. We would like to fit the road version of that engine with a manual gearbox, in a body that takes its vents and its width from the race car.

Engine
S65 V8, road specification
Gearbox
Manual
Body
Racing-inspired package: bonnet, arches, bumpers, diffuser
Priorities
Low weight, quick response and the sound of the V8

This is what we would like to build. There are no power, weight or performance figures yet because the engineering has not been done.

Settled

  • Nothing yet. This study comes after Project 97.

Still to work out

  • Which Z4 to start from. It is drawn here on the E89, and we are also considering the earlier E86 coupé.
  • Whether the S65 V8 and a manual gearbox will fit
  • How the body is made
  • Every dimension except the wheelbase
  • Weight, performance and cost
