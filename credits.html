Notes and credits

Please note

Revival Revolutions is an independent design studio. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by BMW AG, Aston Martin Lagonda or McLaren Automotive. Make and model names appear only to identify the original cars and their racing history.

Every drawing, render and film on this site is concept work. None of the cars has been built, engineered or tested, and the dimensions come from the design and have not been measured on a car. Nothing here is an offer to sell or build a car.
