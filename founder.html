Porsche Cup Suisse

Alvaro raced in Porsche Cup Suisse in 2023 and 2024. It confirmed that racing is where his passion lies, and showed him the canyon between road cars and race cars: they may look alike, but the experience is completely different.

Royal Restauración

His first business: bespoke restoration using race-style fabrication, CAD design and 3D printing to bring old gems back to life, or to turn them into the vision of their owners.

Ram Motorsport Racing ↗

After Porsche, Alvaro built his own team from the ground up: RMR, the first collegiate racing team in New York City, racing Mazda MX-5s. Race wins in its debut season, the championship lead, and two BMW E36 builds now underway.
