Your idea

Have a car in mind?

If there is a racing car you would like to see as a road car, tell us about it.

Concept image

The four studies on this site started as ideas like that. We are happy to hear yours, but Project 97 comes first. A conversation about another car is not a commitment to develop it.

Starting points

Where an idea can come from

  • Another car

    A racing car we have not drawn, and the road car it was based on. We can discuss it as a future design direction, not another active build programme.

  • Your take on Project 97

    A colour, a material or a detail that makes the E36 feel like yours. Changes to the engine or body would need their own engineering review.

  • Something you remember

    It does not have to start with a race car. A photograph, a particular journey or a car you grew up around can explain what matters to you.

What happens next

  1. Stage I

    You tell us the idea

    It can be rough. One sentence is enough.

  2. Stage II

    We talk it through

    Which racing car, which road car, and whether it is something we should be drawing.

  3. Stage III

    We might draw it

    If we take it on, it gets the same treatment as the others, with design drawings first and renders after.

  4. Stage IV

    No commitment either way

    This is a conversation about design. We have not built a car yet, and Project 97 will be the first, so we cannot quote for a build or promise one.

Any idea is worth a first conversation.

What we can offer today is time and design work. If we do not think an idea suits us, we will tell you and explain why.
