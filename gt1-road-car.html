The GT1 Road Car

Proposed · V12 · Manual under study

Concept render Nothing shown here has been built yet.

The DBR9 won its class at Le Mans two years running. This is a DB9 that borrows some of it back.

Summary

The short version

The racer
The DBR9, which won the GT1 class at Le Mans in 2007 and 2008.
What we change
A louvred bonnet, wider vented wings, a front splitter, a rear lip built into the tail and a diffuser with strakes.
What it is for
The V12 of the DB9 with a sharper response and, if it can be done, a manual gearbox.
Settled
Nothing yet. Of the four, this one is the furthest off.
What exists
Renders, design drawings, and cabin and colour studies. No blueprint or engineering yet.

Background

Two June nights at Le Mans

These photographs show the original cars and were taken by other people. Each one is credited underneath.

Launched in 2004 and based on the DB9, the V12-powered DBR9 was built for GT1. It took the class win at the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 2007.

It won again in 2008, when the number 009 car, now in Gulf colours, finished first in GT1 after fighting Corvette for the whole race.

The road DB9 already had the shape. We have widened it and added the racer's vented bonnet, and left the long profile and the clean rear deck alone.

The car

Keeping the V12

The DB9 keeps its V12. We would like it to respond and sound more like the race car, and we are looking at whether the manual gearbox from the V12 Vantage could be fitted. The aero parts are our own design.

Engine
DB9 V12, retained and developed
Gearbox

Body

Profile
The DB9's long side and clean rear deck

This is what we would like to build. There are no power, weight or performance figures yet because the engineering has not been done.
